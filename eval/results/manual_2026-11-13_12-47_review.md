## Numbers

- Right page in top 5 (answerable questions): **11.5 / 16**
- Same 5 questions, German doc: asked in German **4 / 5**, asked in English **1.5 / 5** ← biggest weakness
- Correct refusals on unanswerable questions: **4 / 4**, and no made-up answers
- Fully correct answers: **7 / 16** (8 if you count E4, right content but wrong page cited)
- Retrieval ~40 ms, LLM 3–7 s per question on GPU

## Findings

1. **Cross-language retrieval is weak.** English questions about German documents mostly pull English chunks (the AI study guide) instead of the German source.
2. **Short, table-like pages get lost.** The master flyer's "KEY FACTS" box (4 semesters, 120 ECTS, English) never made the top 5.
3. **Citations are sometimes wrong** (wrong page, or a fact from one doc cited as another).
4. **The 7B model sometimes ignores rules**: answer language, the "nothing else" rule after a refusal.
5. **Good news:** when it doesn't find something, it says so instead of inventing an answer.

## Ideas to try (one at a time, re-run, compare)

- Bigger embedding model: multilingual-e5-base (or bge-m3)
- Translate the question into German as well and search with both
- Hybrid search (BM25 keywords + vectors) and/or a reranker
- Stricter prompt for answer language and citations
