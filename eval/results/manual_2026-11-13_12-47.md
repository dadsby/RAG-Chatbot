## 3. What is the StEOP and how do I complete it?

StEOP stands for Studies Introductory and Orientation Phase. To complete it, you need to collect at least 9 ECTS credits from a specified pool of courses, which means getting a positive grade in these courses. This is important because some core courses are essential for your further studies, and it would be counterproductive to delay them to later semesters. [Bachelor_AI_Studienleitfaden.pdf, p. 29]
