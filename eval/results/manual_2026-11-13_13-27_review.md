## Conclusion

A bigger embedding model did **not** fix the cross-language problem. English questions still pull English chunks
(the AI study guide) over the German source. So the problem isn't model size, it's language bias:
the embeddings match same-language text more strongly. Small answer improvements are probably just from
slightly different context ordering. That's a useful negative result.
